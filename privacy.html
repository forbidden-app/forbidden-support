Forbidden!

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-28

Forbidden! is a single-device party word game. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information.

What we don't collect

  • Personal information of any kind
  • Email addresses or names
  • Contact information
  • Usage analytics
  • Crash reports
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers beyond those iOS uses internally

What is stored on your device

The app stores your local game settings on your device using Apple's UserDefaults system. This data never leaves your device. It includes:

  • Round duration preference
  • Maximum passes per round
  • Sound on/off
  • Taboo penalty on/off
  • Whether you have completed the onboarding tutorial

Third-party services

Forbidden! does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

Children's privacy

Forbidden! is appropriate for children of all ages. Because we do not collect any data from anyone, we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above and post the changes on this page.
